Oakwood Residences

Oakwood Residences is BuildRight Construction's flagship residential development — a modern 12-unit complex designed to redefine urban living in São Paulo. Each unit features open-plan layouts, premium finishes, and smart-home integration, set within a landscaped community environment.

The project prioritizes sustainability with solar panels, rainwater harvesting, and energy-efficient insulation, achieving a Green Building certification. Completed on schedule in 2024, Oakwood Residences stands as a testament to our commitment to quality and innovation.
